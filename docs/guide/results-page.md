# Results page

### Auth Pattern

- **Exclusion reasons and the private notice (S10 WP5a, D2, `TODO(kim-validar:D2)`)**: `lib/exclusionReasons.ts` offers a fixed list for NEW exclusions (`sample_error`, `protocol`, `incomplete`, `not_fit` = "no estaba en condiciones de catar", never a health word, `test_duplicate`, `other` + a required free text of at most 120 chars) and NEVER "scoring differently"; `setParticipantExclusion(sessionId, userId, excluded, reason, reasonText)` stores the code, or `other:<text>` in the existing text column (no schema change); the retired `calibration` and any unknown stored value still render (`exclusionReasonText`). The excluded cupper sees ONE private line on their own results page, built by the pure `ownExclusionNotice` (`lib/exclusionNotice.ts`) from the viewer's own row only, so no other cupper's exclusion reaches a participant's payload (tested); never by email, never in a PDF or CSV. Outlier cells (2 SD or more) say "far from the group, not an error" and use the warning token, not the error one.

### The results views (OR-D, the owner's review of 2026-11-21): read this before the R2 to R5 sections
- **Two VIEWS of the same cupping, named by their perspective** (`docs/audits/app-ui-ux-2026-11-15/RESULTS-VIEWS-PROPOSAL.md`, approved): **"Por café"** for everyone and **"Por catador"** for the organizer and the admin's lens (it is R5's Panel, unchanged inside). They replace the "Resultados | Panel" tabs: the same `PillTabs`, the same ids (`resumen` / `panel`) and the same `?tab=panel`. A participant has one view and no tab bar. What waits for the organizer in "Por catador" is the COUNT on its tab (`badge`, with `badgeLabel` = `pendingSentence(...)` read out in place of the bare number); the "Pendiente en el panel" tile is gone.
- **The rule (OVERENGINEERING-LOG): nothing is said twice in one view.** A coffee's wheel is on its card only; its score is in the ranking box only (a card prints no score; the one exception, the owner's choice: the first coffee's score also heads "Primera del ranking"); a reference has no tile (its score is the ranking's dashed line, said once in its legend; "Comparar con" sits under the ranking when there are several references).
- **"Por café", in the owner's order** (`SessionOverview`, which now takes the cards as `children`): (1) two boxes: "Primera del ranking" = the coffee's NAME and SCORE only (as tall as its content; not drawn when no coffee can be named first: the ranking then says why), and the ranking on its fixed scale; (2) "Café por café" (the cards); (3) "Comparar" (`OverviewCompare`, a section that opens with `PaperSectionHeading` like "Café por café": only once CLOSED, only coffees this viewer may see, the territories over the dim family shades, the table BESIDE the wheel from 768 px through `FlavorCompare`'s `beside`, no line legend because each coffee's letter is on its territory); (4) the small facts that repeat nothing, each opening with its own headline number: "Tú y el panel", "Quiénes cataron" ("k de n" who sent of those who joined, then the excluded; the included count is on the ranking's rows) and "Tazas con defecto" (the total of cups); in the admin's read-only lens nothing is drawn as "tú" (`own={!isAdminViewer}`: no ring in the ranking, no "Tú y el panel"); (5) the reflection. A descriptive session has no boxes.
- **Removed by the owner**: "Aprendizajes" (`Learnings`, `lib/reports/learnings*.ts`, `learningSentences.ts`, their words and tests; `buildDefectSummary` moved to `lib/reports/defectSummary.ts`), the "Each coffee" view and its switch, and **"no clear difference" everywhere on the page** (the bar, its legend and explanation, the chips, the sentence; `tiesWithNext` / `tieBars`, `ScoreContext.noClearGap`, `OverviewFirst.tieWith`). The ranking orders the scores and claims nothing about neighbours: **coffees are compared in "Comparar"**. `hasNoClearGap` and `RankedEntry.noClearGap` stay in `lib/reports/ranking.ts` only because the participant-summary PDF still reads them (the exports package's file).
- The comparison's ground is the dim family shades everywhere on the page (the overview's "Comparar" and the sheet's "Comparar con"): `FlavorCompare` has no `ground` option any more. The PDF's `PdfFlavorCompare` keeps its own (the exports package's file).
- The reflection is still one editor per coffee (`components/results/Reflection.tsx`, words in `results.reflection`); the general reflection per tasting is the next package (it needs a table and a migration).
- Tests: `tests/resultsViews.test.ts` (the two views, the tab's count, what was removed), `tests/resultsOverview.test.ts` (the view's order, nothing twice).

### The results overview (R2, 2026-11)
- The first tab of the results page is the OVERVIEW: tiles, general first (since OR-D: the coffee the panel put first and the ranking on one fixed scale; then, after the cards and "Comparar", the reader against the panel, who cupped, the defective cups). It replaced `ResumenTab` (stat row, "Mejor muestra", "Mi desempeño"). Design: `docs/audits/app-ui-ux-2026-11-15/RESULTS-DESIGN.md` sections 2 and 4.
- **One pure model, two functions** (`lib/reports/overview.ts`, tested state by state in `tests/resultsOverview.test.ts`): `buildOverviewData` runs ON THE SERVER (`results/page.tsx`) and returns counts and aggregates only (per sample the wheel's counts through `buildWheelData` / `buildOwnWheelData` and the defective cups through `buildDefectSummary`; for the session, who cupped); `overviewView` turns the samples the page already ships plus that data into the tiles, in the browser, because the reference a viewer compares against is chosen there. Neither adds a rule: who may read the group's numbers is still the page's ONE `viewerMayReadGroupResults` call (`canViewGroup`), and the group's rows are passed to `buildOverviewData` ONLY under it (`rows: canViewGroup && session.isGroup ? ... : null`); otherwise everything is built from the viewer's own evaluations.
- **What a participant's payload may hold**: the keys of `OverviewData` are counts (`n`, `direct`, `rollup`, `cuppers`, `cups`, `joined`, `sent`, `included`); never a cupper's key, name, free text or value. `who.excluded` (how many participants are excluded) is the ORGANIZER'S lens only; a participant gets null. A test serialises the payload of every scenario and scans it.
- **Rules it keeps, each from its one place**: no own total for an unsent draft (`myScoreFor` -> `canShowMyScore`); references outside the ranking, the average and "first" (`rankSamples`); no "first", spread or difference under three included cuppers (`scoreContext` / `panelGates`); the wheel hidden from a participant under `FLAVOR_WHEEL_MIN_N` (`too_few`) and absent in an affective session (`no_wheel`: no empty wheel, no word about descriptors); a participant's own scores before the close are in CUPPING order with no places (not a ranking); "79.00" and "fija" only where `referenceExplanation` says pinned. A score is ink: the numeral takes the text colour, and the error red appears only on the mark of a defective cup.
- **Two cuppers (audit A-02, accepted by the owner's decision 6)**: a participant of a two-cupper session sees the group's score beside their own, as before this package, and so can work out the other person's total. What needs three: the spread, "first", "diferencia", "Tú y el panel", the group's flavour wheel.
- The page's title is the session's name (one `h1`, through `PageHeader`) with one line of facts under it; who cupped is said once, in its tile (not in the header nor in a notice over the overview).
- Fixtures: `lib/dev/overviewFixtures.ts` builds invented cuppings through the real `computeGroupAggregate` and `buildOverviewData`; the dev board (`/dev/results-parts?only=overview`, development only) shows the states no test account can (a participant looking at a closed group cupping, two cuppers, a free reference).

### The coffee cards and "Comparar" (R2 step 2, 2026-11)
- Under the overview, ONE CARD PER COFFEE (`components/results/CoffeeCards.tsx`): references first and marked "Fuera del ranking", then the ranking's order. They replaced the table of 17 columns (`ScoreTable`), the radar cards (`SampleRadarChart`) and the Tabla / Gráfico switch (`cata_results_view`), all deleted. A card opens the coffee's sheet (`SampleDetailDialog`, R3). The page's views are "Por café" and "Por catador" (OR-D); with one view there is no tab bar.
- **One pure model** (`lib/reports/coffeeCards.ts`, tested in `tests/coffeeCards.test.ts`): `coffeeCards` takes the order, the places and everything said beside a score from `overviewView` (a card can never disagree with its tile), and adds the viewer's own state (`none | draft | submitted`, declared partial), the wheel with the three most chosen descriptors (`countedDescriptors`) and the viewer's marks, and `qualityRows` (the viewer's value per quality attribute under `canShowMyRatings`; the group's only under `ctx.canViewGroup` and `panelGates(...).groupQualityByAttribute`, two included cuppers). It adds no gate: the wheel's counts come from the server-built `OverviewData`. A test serialises what the cards add for a participant and scans it, and pins the set of fields.
- **What a card shows**: place and label, the name once revealed, "En curso" / "Sin evaluar" (the viewer's own row) and "Parcial · sin puntaje", no score (OR-D: the scores are in the ranking), the wheel + "Lo más elegido" (own scope: "Lo que marcaste", names only; a participant under three cuppers: one sentence), and in an AFFECTIVE session the eight quality attributes as a small list instead of a wheel. "Revelar" is on the card only for the organizer of a cupping that is still open (`revealSample` is refused after the close).
- **Phones (under 768 px, `PHONE_MAX`)**: a CSS scroll-snap row (cards 82 % wide, the next one showing), NUMBERED steps under it as real 44 px buttons (never dots), and the row is ONE Tab stop: a roving tabindex on the cards' cover buttons, arrow keys / Home / End move between cards. From 768 px it is a grid and every card is its own stop.
- **"Comparar"** (`OverviewCompare`; a section of its own since OR-D, no "Cada café" switch): only once the session is CLOSED and only coffees whose counts this viewer may see (`compareOptions` over `wheelCompareInput`); up to three coffees, the first three of the ranking to start, each with the letter of its label (numbers when two labels would share a letter). Under the territories: the dim family shades (OR-D; no option).
- **Deliberately gone with the table** (the CSV in "Exportar" has the full table): the group's average per attribute when ONE cupper is included (it is one person's ratings; the organizer has the matrix), "Revelar" on a closed session (it was refused by the server), the legend "mine / community" of the table.

### The coffee's sheet (R3, 2026-11)
- A card opens the coffee's SHEET: `SampleDetailDialog` (a bottom sheet on phones, a dialog from 640 px; the organizer's cupper switch, "Asignar café", "Editar muestra", "Editar datos de origen") around `SampleDetail` (the body). It replaced the old drill-down's collapsible blocks with filled pills, the temporary "El grupo en este café" block, `SampleComparisons`, and the whole Descriptores tab (`DescriptoresTab`, `WordCloud`, `lib/wordCloud.ts`, `DescriptorFrequency` with its roll-up of typed words and its sentences), all deleted. The results page's views are "Por café" and, for the organizer, "Por catador"; a participant has no tab bar.
- **Order of the sheet** (RESULTS-DESIGN.md section 3): 1 the score (named in full once, `ScoreWithContext`, the arithmetic line, ONE "¿Cómo se calculó?" through `ScoreBreakdownPanel`, which now takes `individual` and/or `group` parts; the group's part says "grupo", never "comunidad"); 2 quality on a fixed 1 to 9 `DotPlot` (the reader's ring, the group's dot; a fixed reference gets a sentence instead); 3 what was found (the full `FlavorWheel`, "Comparar con" = `FlavorCompare` with this coffee as `base`, the descriptors by people stage by stage, what the reader marked); 4 intensities on a fixed 0 to 15 `DotPlot`; 5 defective cups by type; 6 the same coffee twice (B6); 7 notes, the note after the close, origin and prep data.
- **Gates, each from its one place** (the sheet adds none): the score's context and the quality rows are the card's (`coffeeCards`: the group's value per quality attribute under `ctx.canViewGroup` and `panelGates(...).groupQualityByAttribute`, TWO included cuppers, the owner's decision 6; this replaced the three-per-row rule of B3 for QUALITY only); the group's stored numbers reach the sheet only when `isGroup && canViewGroup`; intensities and the shared-descriptors sentences are still `buildPersonalComparison` (closed session, participant lens, three included cuppers per row, never the organizer's lens); the wheel, the defects and the per-stage lists are built on the server. `buildSheetData` (`lib/reports/coffeeSheet.ts`) gives a coffee's lists ONLY where that coffee's wheel reached the viewer as the group's counts (so: not to a participant under three cuppers, which the old Descriptores tab did show; not before the close; not in a solo or affective session) and lists only ids that resolve in the reference data, never a typed word. `descriptorFrequency` no longer leaves the server. While the organizer reads ONE cupper (`cupperName`), no group prop is passed at all. Tests: `tests/coffeeSheet.test.ts`.
- **A family's colour is a dot beside a word** (H-01): the sheet has no pill filled with a flavour colour and no coloured text; `tests/coffeeSheet.test.ts` scans for it. `components/results/**` and the results route are OUT of the alias allowlist of `tests/appTokens.test.ts` (no alias class, no `bg-white`, no legacy radius there).
- **The address**: `?sample=<session sample id>`, pure rules in `lib/sheetAddress.ts`, browser part in `results/useSheetAddress.ts` over the native `history.pushState` / `replaceState` (Next 16 integrates them with `useSearchParams`; no server round trip). Opening pushes; closing is `history.back()` when this visit pushed, else a replace (reload, shared link); an unknown id opens nothing and is removed. The dialog stays MOUNTED while closed (`open={detailSample !== null}`) so `ResponsiveDialog` can note where focus was and give it back to the card. Which cupper the organizer reads is local state, not in the address.
- **Deliberately gone with the Descriptores tab**: the word cloud (it counted mentions), the roll-up by family with typed words, the summary sentences, "hide single-person descriptors" (`cata_results_hide_singles`; the lists are ranked by people and show eight rows first), one cupper's descriptors across ALL coffees at once (the organizer reads a cupper coffee by coffee through the sheet's switch). The alignment table moved to the Panel unchanged, with its own coffee filter.

### The organizer's Panel (R5, 2026-11)
- The second tab of the results page, for the organizer (and the admin's read-only lens) of a group session with participants, is ONE view, `components/results/PanelView.tsx`, in the order the organizer works in: (1) the evaluations received late (`LateEvaluationsPanel`, the organizer only), (2) "Análisis por catador": every cupper's total for every coffee beside the group's score and the spread (`PanelMatrix`), and under it who counts in the group's numbers (the exclusion switches, each a 44 px target), (3) the notes the cuppers added after the close, (4) the alignment. It ARRANGES and REDRAWS; no control changed: `setParticipantExclusion` with its reason list and `ExclusionDialog`, `acceptLateEvaluation` with its version guard and its "recibida después del cierre" label, the notes read-only, a cell opening that cupper's evaluation through the sheet's cupper lens.
- **One pure model** (`lib/reports/panel.ts`, tested in `tests/panel.test.ts`): `buildPanelMatrix` (a cupper's total through `calcIndividualScore` under the evaluation's own rule, the spread and each cell's distance through `rowStats` / `bandFor` over the INCLUDED cuppers, unchanged numbers; the "Grupo" line is the score each coffee's CARD shows, passed in, so the two can never disagree), `panelPending` (what the count on the "Por catador" tab adds up), `alignmentBars`. It adds no gate: the page builds `participants`, the late rows, the other cuppers' notes and the alignment rows only in the organizer's lens, so a participant's page holds none of them (a test pins those four conditions in `page.tsx` and that the Panel is mounted in one place under `canViewIndividual`).
- **Distance is a tone AND a mark** (audit H-09): within 1 SD `primary-fixed`, 1 SD or more `warning-container` with a triangle, 2 SD or more `warning` with a diamond; a far cell also says so in its accessible name ("lejos del grupo, 2 DE o más"). Far from the group is not an error: never an error token (DESIGN.md "How far from the group" says the same since this package). An excluded cupper is greyed and has no distance. Under three included totals for a coffee NO cell has a distance (`PANEL_DISTANCE_MIN_N` = `RANKING_POLICY.minN`): with two totals each sits exactly one SD from their mean, so the band would mark both; the spread is still printed from two, and `bandFor` (shared with the ficha técnica) is untouched.
- **Layout**: under 768 px one coffee at a time (a `PillTabs` picker, then the cuppers as a list: no sideways scroll); from 768 px a real table (hidden caption, `th scope`, a focusable scroll region, and a line under it only while it is wider than its box). `PANEL_MATRIX_ROWS` (`lib/reports/panel.ts`) is the ONE line that picks the table's direction: `"cuppers"` (built: a row per cupper, a column per coffee, the group's score and the spread as the first two rows; a class of thirty is thirty rows) or `"coffees"` (the old direction and the design's "Grupo column"; it scrolls sideways from about eight cuppers at 768 px). The choice is the owner's.
- **The alignment is one neutral bar per cupper** (audit A-05, DESIGN.md "An alignment is one neutral bar"): "k de o" and the percentage in ink, one tone for every included cupper, a quieter one for an excluded cupper; the coffee filter lives in `CupperAlignment` itself.
- **What waits in the Panel** is the count on the "Por catador" tab (OR-D; the "Pendiente en el panel" tile is gone), only when something waits: the organizer's count adds the late evaluations and the notes after the close, the admin's read-only lens counts the notes it holds and never a late evaluation; a screen reader hears "1 evaluación tardía por decidir y 2 notas después del cierre". A page that holds late rows and has no Panel (it cannot happen today: late rows need other cuppers) still shows the block, so a late evaluation can never become unreachable. The words R5 added are `results.panel` (`panelStrings.ts`); everything the Panel already said keeps its keys. **`offline.panel` is another namespace's key of the same name**: a script that edits the message files as text must look for `"panel"` only inside `"results"` (one that did not deleted `offline.panel` during this package; caught by the capture run, not by a test).

### Tabs, steps and place (P5a, 2026-11)
- Tabs INSIDE a page are `PillTabs` (a tablist: one Tab stop, arrow keys / Home / End move and select, `idBase` + `TabPanel` tie a tab to its panel); a choice of how to draw the same data is `SegmentedControl`, a RADIO GROUP with the same keys; tabs BETWEEN pages are `RouteTabs` (links with `aria-current="page"`, an edge fade with a chevron when the row scrolls sideways). The keyboard rule is ONE pure function, `nextRovingIndex` (`lib/rovingFocus.ts`). No selected state uses the action fill.
- **Place lives in the address** (`lib/viewAddress.ts`, pure, tested in `tests/tabsAndPlace.test.ts`): the results tab (`?tab=panel`, coexisting with R3's `?sample=`; a tab the viewer cannot have is removed) and every `DataTable`'s search / filters / sort / page (`q`, `f-<facet>`, `sort=<key>.<dir>`, `page`; `addressKey` prefixes them, `false` opts out; `components/ui/useTablePlace.ts` reads once at mount and writes with `history.replaceState`, search after 300 ms because Safari caps history writes). A prefix used by two mounted tables is written by the first only (dev warning). `LocaleSwitchLink` already keeps the query.

### Personal comparisons and the repeated sample (S10 WP5b, closed sessions only)

Neutral wording: never "error", no red/green. No Δ was added (it stays where the page already shows it).
