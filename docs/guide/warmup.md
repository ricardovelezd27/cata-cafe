- **One timeline, one clock.** `lib/warmup/timeline.ts` (`buildExerciseTimeline`, pure, vitest golden fixtures in `tests/warmup-timeline.test.ts`) turns an exercise into ms segments `arrive(40 s) → [read → step…] → closing(30 s) [→ bridge 5 s in session mode]`. A READ BEAT precedes every step and THE VOICE IS ITS CLOCK (`readBeatMs`): the bell's head start (`VOICE_AFTER_CUE_MS` 700 ms) + the measured length of the recorded instruction (the longer of es and en, so a mixed room shares one timeline) + `READ_TAIL_MS` 800 ms, rounded up to 250 ms, never under 4 s nor over 12 s (an instruction that would need more fails a test instead of being capped); a line with no recording falls back to an estimate. So absolute times after the arrival move when the voice is re-rendered: the fixtures name a pause "beat" and a moment by its segment (`tests/helpers/warmupRows.ts`), never by its millisecond; `holdBeforeSec` folds into it; every segment starts where the previous one ended (right-left round trips without `returns` go extreme → extreme — Kim Q1 pending). `hooks/useRunClock.ts` (pure reducer in `lib/warmup/runClock.ts`) is the single clock: `elapsedMs()` is non-reactive, `seek`/`setStopAt`/`runId` drive Prev/Next, "Quieto" mode and the synced run. **Never add a second clock** (CSS keyframes, GSAP, a per-phase countdown).
- **Rendering**: `components/warmup/WarmupPlayer.tsx` runs one rAF loop (30 fps cap, interval fallback for occluded windows) that writes SVG `transform` ATTRIBUTES on the figure's `[data-part]` groups (`lib/warmup/poses.ts`), `--p` (= elapsed / total of the WHOLE run) on the single-track `StepProgress` (`[data-bar-fill]`, halo `[data-halo]` — one bar, no per-segment gaps or hold dots since 2026-10-29), `data-passed` on trajectory dots and `StageField.draw(state, tMs, unwatched, snap)` — the dots of the stage (the landing's constellation language, source-over green/terracotta on the light stage, additive mint on dusk) are drawn from the SAME loop, never their own rAF (see "The stage's dots" below). The cue text is keyed by its TEXT, not the segment, so read → hold → leg never re-animates the same line. Eyes-closed `[data-dusk]` also portals a fixed `.duskVeil` to `<body>` (z 59, opacity .94) and lifts the inline root to z 60, so on desktop the app shell darkens too. React state changes only on segment change. Figures (`components/warmup/figures/`) are static soft BARISTA silhouettes (hair bun, apron + bean mark, calm face; pivots in `poses.ts` GEO unchanged): no CSS transforms/keyframes on `[data-part]` groups (they would compound with the attribute pivots); stage colours come from `--wu-*` variables set by the player (dusk set on `[data-dusk]`), read through `--fig-*` fallbacks.
- **Sound** (`hooks/useWarmupAudio.ts`, recipes in `lib/warmup/sounds.ts`): four cues with ONE meaning each — *campana* = start of a movement, *tic* = end of a movement / start of a read beat, *cuenco* = closing starts, *doble* = open your eyes — pre-scheduled on the AudioContext clock from `timeline.cues` at play/seek/resume, cancelled on pause. `unlock()` must run inside the Empezar gesture. Every movement unit is *campana → hum → tic*: `timeline.hums` has ONE window per leg / reps / rest / return cross (lead-in included) plus the arrival breaths (which also get campana/tic), each with a `contour` the hum's pitch follows — `rise` for up/right/forward, `fall` for down/left/back/centre, `wave` under fast reps, `breath` (4 s up / 6 s down) on "Llega". `renderHum` is a low root doubled L/R a few Hz apart with its 2nd to 4th harmonics carrying the energy (so a phone speaker reproduces it; the first tone, 132 Hz behind a 640 Hz lowpass, was inaudible there), swell + vibrato LFOs, low-passed; a leg blooms over its last ~2 s and closes before the tic (`HUM.arrive`); each exercise has ONE root note and brightness (`TONES` / `toneFor`), nothing else differs. The tone is silent on holds and read beats; the AIR (below) is not. Breath pacing only on "Llega"; legs and the closing are never breath-paced.
- **A stop the clock makes by itself rings out (2026-11, delight pass PR 1)**: `audio.schedule(cues, elapsed, hums, { untilMs, afterMs })` schedules ONE stretch only, up to the point where the clock stops by itself (`totalMs`, or the step end / `arriveMs` in "Quieto" mode); pure rules in `lib/warmup/audioWindow.ts` (`dueCues`, `dueHums`, `ringsOut`, `resumeAfterStop`; `tests/warmup-audioWindow.test.ts`). When the clock gets there (`RunClockEvent` "stop" or "done") the player does NOT call `audio.cancel()` / `cancelSpeech()`: the doble that ends a library run (scheduled exactly at `totalMs`) and the tic of a Quieto step end finish sounding. Only a stop the person asked for (pause, seek, exit, hidden tab) cancels. A cue that sits exactly on a stop plays at the stop and is not repeated on resume (`afterMs`); a narration line that starts on a Quieto stop waits for the resume (`narrUntil`). Never schedule past `untilMs`, and never add a cancel on the natural-stop path. **Sound for one run**: a host default (`soundDefault`, a follower of a group run starts muted) goes through `audio.setMuted(m, { persist: false })` and never writes `cata_warmup_sound`; only the person's own toggle is stored. **Unlock in the tap**: `unlockWarmupAudio()` (module-level export of `hooks/useWarmupAudio.ts`) must be called synchronously in the gesture when the player mounts later (the master's "start warm-up" in `CupClient` awaits a server action first; without it iOS stays silent); a synced player also runs the resume check once at mount.
- **The mix (2026-11, delight pass PR 3)**: `lib/warmup/mix.ts` (pure, `tests/warmup-mix.test.ts`) holds the six numbers of `MIX`, each written RELATIVE TO THE GUIDE VOICE (`markersDb`, `toneDb`, `airDb`, `duckDb`, `duckAttackMs`, `duckReleaseMs`); they are the owner's. **They were set by MEASUREMENT on 2026-11-14, not by ear** (he could not listen before it shipped and asked for it to be made right without him): ear-weighted dB(A) levels, on full-range playback and through a 400 Hz high-pass phone-speaker model, kept as tests ("perceived levels of the committed mix": the bell's strike 14 to 26 dB above the tone, the tone at least 8 dB more present on a phone than the old one, the air at least 6 dB under the tone, the bells within 6 dB of their level before the mixer, `FALLBACK_VOICE_VOLUME` turned down by the same amount as the bells). Nobody has confirmed them by ear yet; when he does, the numbers change and those tests are re-read, not deleted. The hard limits stay (background at least 12 dB under the voice while a line is spoken, worst-case sum within 3 dB of full scale, markers / tone / air alone never reach the limiter). `laneGains` turns them into gains from each recipe's OWN nominal level (`humRmsDb`, `cueAttackRmsDb`, `airRmsDb` in `sounds.ts`), so a retune keeps "x dB under the voice" true: never hard-code a lane gain. Graph in `hooks/useWarmupAudio.ts`: voice, markers and a background lane (tone + air) into ONE safety limiter (`LIMITER`, threshold near full scale on purpose: a DynamicsCompressor adds make-up gain that grows as the threshold drops); the voice, the limiter and the background lane are persistent, each `schedule()` hangs three short-lived gain nodes on them and `cancel()` fades those. **Ducking is drawn ahead, never a sidechain**: `duckingPlan(speechWindows, elapsed, mix)` gives straight ramps on the background lane's gain (down exactly when the first word starts, back one release after the last; lines closer than a release + an attack stay ducked, no pumping). **Air** (`renderAir`): soft filtered noise for the whole stretch, the one sound that means "the session is running"; not tonal on purpose. The phone's own voice plays outside the graph: its windows are estimated (`estimateSpeechMs`) and its level trimmed (`FALLBACK_VOICE_VOLUME`). When muted nothing is scheduled (unchanged). Dev: `/app/warmup/dev-player` has a "dev · mix" panel (`setWarmupMixOverride`, `playWarmupVoiceClip` with `public/warmup/dev/voice-reference.mp3`); it overrides `MIX` for that page only and saves nothing.
- **The stage's dots mean the instruction (2026-11, delight pass PR 7)**: `components/warmup/StageField.tsx` (it replaced `BreathField`) is mounted for the WHOLE run, behind the figure. WHAT the dots do is the pure `lib/warmup/stageField.ts` (`fieldStateAt`, tested in `tests/warmup-stageField.test.ts` against every real timeline: five exercises, both modes, both lengths, "poco espacio" on and off): the arrival is a disc that widens on the inhale and gathers on the exhale; during a leg the cloud travels WITH the body part (`poseOffset`: the same direction on screen as the figure's extremity, arriving exactly when the figure does, and 1.2 times longer along the line of the movement); a hold gathers and goes still over 0.9 s; stillness is a small quiet cloud; the closing settles into one ring; the bridge tightens the ring to a cup seen from above. The centre of the cloud is a plain function of the POSE the figure shows, so the field has no memory and no clock: pause, seek or join late and it is right. During a step the dots recede (`FIELD.behindFigure`) because the figure is the instruction. **It is a second HOST of the landing's particle language, not a second engine**: the seed, the frame-rate-independent easing (`frameEase`), the idle drift (`driftX` / `driftY`), the dot size (`dotSize`, from the viewport, so a dot is the same size on the landing and here) and the colour parsing live in `lib/canvas/particles.ts`, the draw-cost governor in `lib/canvas/governor.ts`, and `ConstellationCanvas` uses the same ones (`tests/canvasParticles.test.ts` fails if either canvas keeps a private copy). **Budget** (`FIELD_BUDGET`, decided by the pure `fieldDrawDecision`): at most 30 draws a second whatever the loop's rate, about 10 while the eyes-closed screen has its controls hidden (`unwatched` = dusk and `chromeHidden`), no draw at all when the picture would not change (dots settled, no drift, same state: so reduced motion is one draw per change of the figure, and in the arrival only the light follows the breath); 300 dots on a phone, 520 on a wide screen, times 0.6 on a weak device; no gradient and no colour string made per frame (the pool of light is a CSS layer moved with transform and opacity); the governor cuts the dots twice at a mean draw cost over 4 ms, then switches the field off until the page reloads. Never give the field a loop or a timer of its own, and never make the dots do something the instruction does not say.
