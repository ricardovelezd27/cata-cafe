# Design and landing

### The arrival stage (S8 of the 2026-11-15 audit, 2026-11)
- Sign-in, the emailed link's button page, the guest claim card and the two join pages render inside `ArrivalStage` (`components/arrival/ArrivalStage.tsx`): the landing's dark ground (`--color-stage` to `--color-stage-deep`, one still pool of mint light behind the card, no canvas, nothing that moves), the shared `Wordmark` (`components/brand/Wordmark.tsx`, also in the landing header), the language as a link to the same page in the other language, ONE solid cream card (`.surface-solid`, 400 px) and the legal links under it (only while `LEGAL_PAGES_PUBLISHED`). The page's `<main>` and its one `h1` are in the shell and the card.
- **It never takes `.stage-ground`**: `app/globals.css` reads that class as "this is the landing" and turns off the paper focus ring, the paper heading rule and the one reduced-motion reset. The ground is painted in `ArrivalStage.module.css` from the same tokens; the focus ring is mint on the ground and the paper green inside the card. `tests/arrivalStage.test.ts` computes every text and control pair from the tokens.
- **Every state is one anatomy**, `ArrivalCard` (`components/arrival/ArrivalCard.tsx`): a tone mark (plain, info, warning, error: the kit's `Notice` tones, each with its icon), the title (the page's `h1`), one or two sentences, the form, then the next step as a full-width button with quieter actions under it. A refused or expired link is `warning` (nobody's mistake), a failure on our side is `error`, "check your email" is `info` (`EmailSentState` with `arrival`). It draws only: roles, live regions, focus targets and ids are passed in by each screen as they were.
- **The sign-in path's logic is untouched by this shell** (actions, `next`, cookies, the confirm button, the code entry, Google, `joinScreenFor` / `evaluateJoin`, `GuestIdentityChoice`'s order, `GuestJoinForm`'s reuse rule, the in-app-browser hint, every message). A change there is not a shell change.
- The dots are not on the arrival screens yet: a still formation needs the landing's engine to learn an unscrolled mode (audit K-03, a later package). The dev board `/dev/arrival?state=sent|identity|guest` shows the states no test account reaches without writing.
- The numbered step on PAPER is ONE recipe, `.step-mark` (`app/globals.css`, beside the stage's `.rail-step`): the coffee cards' row on a phone and the onboarding dialog ("Paso 2 de 3", `onboarding.stepOf`).

## Styling

- **Landing density rules (2026-11-15, owner's review of the live page; DESIGN.md "Density: four rules learned on the landing")**. They are checked on the landing by `tests/landingDensity.test.ts` and are to be applied to the whole app by the UI/UX audit (rubric checks F-7, F-8, D-7, I-6, C-6, C-7). (1) **White space has a ceiling**: every stage section takes `.stage-section` (40 / 48 / 56 px above and below at phone / 640 / 768 px) and `.stage-section-head` (24 / 28 / 36 px to its content); a section never sets its own vertical padding. The pinned steps of "Cómo funciona" are `min-height: 56vh` (the last one `max(56vh, 470px)` so the third screen lands before the pile un-sticks on a 720 px tall laptop; the first starts level with the card), and from 768 px a screen sits beside its text even in the plain layout. The pile's box is 380 px (80 headroom + 300 card; `PILE.footroom` is 0 in `journey/formations.ts`: change both together). (2) **Progress follows the direction of travel**: the rails are vertical and fill downward (`.steps-rail*` between the pile and the texts; `DeckRail` at the LEFT edge of the phone stage), and a counter with a chevron (`.deck-more`, "02 / 05", written by `ScrollDeck` through `[data-deck-now]`, gone on `data-deck-end`) says the next card is below; no bobbing; never a horizontal rail for a vertical scroll. (3) **A first screen has a word budget**: hero title up to 7 words, up to 25 more, four icon chips of up to three words, no word repeated across eyebrow, title and sentence. (4) **An ask appears once**: the reservation form (`WaitlistForm`) is rendered ONLY in `FoundingSection` (`id` from `components/landing/anchors.ts`); the hero card's button is `ScrollToLink` (a real `#link`; with script it lays the sections out with `data-cv-visible`, JUMPS, never smooth-scrolls through the pinned stages, and focuses the field only on `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`). `SectionHeading` is centred at every width (it turned left from 640 px until this date); its children sit at the right end of the title's row from 640 px.
- **Landing, second review (2026-11-15, same day; DESIGN.md "Two more from the second review" and "A step of progress carries its number")**. The owner walked back the three pinned phone sections and asked for a tablet that copies the desktop. (1) **One layout breakpoint, 768 px**: under it the phone layout, from it the desktop layout. `PHONE_MAX` (`components/landing/carouselIndex.ts`, `(max-width: 767.98px)`) is the one constant; `ScrollDeck` (phone stage), `StepsActiveObserver` (`ENHANCED`, pinned pile), `ConstellationCanvas` (`narrow = W < 768`), the `.steps-*` enhanced media query, `.stage-section`'s top step and every `md:` arrangement class of the landing use 768. Never add an arrangement at `sm:` or `lg:` on the landing (`tests/landingDensity.test.ts` scans for `lg:grid-cols`, `sm:grid-cols` and `lg:w-`); between 768 and 1023 px a part that would not fit is set a size down with `md:max-lg:` (section title `2.6rem`, hero numeral `11vw`, feature cell `md:p-4`), never rearranged. The pile's column is `minmax(348px, 9fr)` (`PILE.minColW` in `journey/formations.ts`: change both together); it only bites under about 885 px. (2) **Phones: one scroll stage, two sideways rows.** `ScrollDeck` is used by "Cómo funciona" ONLY (it must be read in order). "Qué incluye" and "Para quién es" are CSS scroll-snap rows (`w-[82vw] max-w-[320px]` cards, the next one showing at the edge) with `CarouselSteps` under them; from 768 px the first is the 3 x 2 grid and the second three cards with `CarouselControls` arrows (rendered through `SectionHeading`'s children, which are shown from 768 px only). Never pin optional cards again, and never give `ScrollDeck` a second section without the owner asking. `CarouselSteps focusable` makes a list a Tab stop only while it is a sideways scroller. (3) **Numbers, not dots**: every progress step is `.rail-step` (a 20 px numbered circle, filled when on): the pinned pile's rail (desktop and tablet), the phone stage's rail and the rows' steps. The desktop rail's steps grew from 10 to 20 px for this; it is the one change at 1024 px and wider.
- **`<html lang>`** is set by an inline script in the ROOT layout (`app/layout.tsx`, from the URL), not under `[locale]`: Next mounts everything under `[locale]` anew when the language changes without a reload, and a `<script>` created by React on the client never runs. `components/landing/LocaleSwitch.tsx` sets the attribute itself after such a change.
