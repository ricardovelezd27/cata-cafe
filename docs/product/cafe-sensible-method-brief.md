## 2. Method structure and the 7 senses
**Three pillars** [V, baristakim.es/metodo-...paso-a-paso; estudiodecafe.com]: Técnica (claridad), Criterio (estabilidad), Sensibilidad (seguridad). Sensibilidad is "the least-trained". Pilar 3 = Sensibilidad is confirmed.
**Core principle** [V]: "Primero sientes. Después describes." / "La mayoría describe para sentir. La cata sensible siente para describir." Also "Percibir antes de interpretar. Sentir antes de explicar."
**Three tastings in order** [V]: afectiva (¿me gusta? score 1-9, then "why") -> descriptiva (name only after feeling) -> extrínseca (origin/process, blind first).
**Workshop sequence** [V]: touch a raisin eyes-closed and name textures; acidity/sweetness with nose plugged ("para aislar un sentido hay que silenciar los demás"); retronasal aroma (release nose, exhale slowly, tongue against palate); relaxation + propiocepción exercise (feet on floor, eyes closed, slow head and gaze movements) before tasting; fragrance (ground) vs aroma (brewed).

**The seven senses** [V: baristakim.es/cata-cafe-sensible-siete-sentidos; Buscalibre/Vaporvell listings]:
1. Vista (sight) 2. Oído (hearing, "escuchar su historia") 3. Olfato 4. Gusto 5. Tacto
6. **Propiocepción** — "la percepción de tu cuerpo en el espacio"; also "la capacidad de sentir tu propio cuerpo y su estado". Lost when tense, along with ability to taste with judgment. Book blurb: "para ubicarnos".
7. **Interocepción** — a "tacto interior": what you perceive inside your body when a coffee makes you feel something. Blurb: "para expresar sus efectos".
Kim says the two added senses are scientifically recognized (not Feldenkrais/vestibular — vestibular is **not** in his list).

## 3. Movimiento Sensorial and principles to honour
Facts [V]: sessions rest on Danis Bois's somato-psicopedagogía (neodrinks interview: "meditación y movimientos lentos"); done **seated, slow, minimal, conscious movements, no physical effort**; goal is to arrive at the cup "con el cuerpo más disponible, más abierto, más presente" (baristakim.es percepcion-sensorial). Preface: "ejercicios de consciencia sensorial, movimientos corporales que ayudan a mejorar atención y concentración"; practised **before** a tasting; claim that 80% of students then perceive and describe aromas correctly. Testimonials: "Menos estímulo puede generar más sensibilidad", "la cata empieza antes de probar el café".
Context [V, baristakim.es/aprender-a-catar-cafe-errores-comunes]: "si vas deprisa, reduces la sensibilidad"; taste seated, slow rhythm; standing adds micro-tension; eyes consume much of attention, so close them while perceiving; "preparar el instrumento. Tú."; shift from "modo social" to "modo percepción"; "cuando el cuerpo se relaja, la percepción se abre"; a few minutes of activation beforehand helps.

MUST honour in-app: slowness; minimal range, never force/stretch or "perform"; seated; interior attention (sensing, not evaluating); sensing precedes words; relaxed body, feet on floor; eyes closed optional; short (minutes).
**Unverified [U]:** the fast->slow->eyes-closed progression, the 15-second trajectory rule, the closing questions "¿Cómo me hace sentir?" / "¿Qué es nuevo para mí?", the "Ritual Café Sensible" name, the "observa" prompts, breath/silence cues, any Feldenkrais influence, and the literal text of the exercises (book ch. 5 and 10 only; the free excerpt PDF contains preface/index only). Only Danis Bois / somato-psicopedagogía is sourced. "¿Cómo me hace sentir?" is consistent with the interoception definition but not found verbatim. ## 4. Design implications (mobile-first guided exercise player)
1. Default to seated, phone set down/propped; open with one line: feet on floor, shoulders loose.
2. One instruction at a time, large calm type, no scrolling; during movement show almost no text — the figure/animation carries it.
3. Slow, fixed pacing; no countdown pressure or streak/score; a visible but soft progress cue (breathing-like ring), no "fail" states.
4. Eyes-closed mode: voice guidance + subtle haptic ticks at phase changes; screen dims; works with screen locked where possible (audio element).
5. Voice: unhurried, tuteo, warm; generous silences (pre-recorded audio, not TTS bursts); a mute/no-voice mode with haptics only.
6. Language: "siente", "nota", "sin esfuerzo", "a tu ritmo"; avoid "debes", "perfecto", evaluative words, and flavour descriptors during the exercise.
7. Minimal-range figures: slow, small-amplitude animations; offer a "stay still, only attend" variant for accessibility/limited mobility.
8. Always skippable/stoppable without penalty; "sin prisa" copy; never block cupping behind the exercise.
9. Place it before the first sample (pre-tasting), 2-5 min; on completion hand off quietly to the cup screen.
10. Reflection capture only after: 1-2 optional open prompts (candidates, pending Kim's confirmation: "¿Cómo me hace sentir?", "¿Qué es nuevo para mí?"), free text, nothing scored, kept separate from CVA data.
11. Keep the tone of the app's reference to 7 senses: show propiocepción/interocepción with Kim's one-line definitions in an info hint.
12. Credit the method (Kim Ossenblok / Café Sensible) and confirm licensing/permission before reproducing his exercises.
