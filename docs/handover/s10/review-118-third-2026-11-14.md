## Findings

### High

**H1. Anyone can move another person's reservation to their own address.**
- Where: `app/actions/auth.ts:362` (writes `user_metadata.email_change_from`), `app/actions/auth.ts:161` (`moveReservationAfterEmailChange(data.user)`), `lib/emailChangeRules.ts:28`.
- Why it works: `user_metadata` can be written by the user. With the public anon key, any signed-in user can call `supabase.auth.updateUser({ email: "new@mine", data: { email_change_from: "victim@x" } })`, or a direct `PUT /auth/v1/user`.
- Scenario: the attacker confirms both of their own addresses. `confirmEmailToken` with `type=email_change` returns a user with no `new_email` and the forged `email_change_from`. `moveWaitlistEntry("victim@x", "new@mine")` then rewrites the victim's founding-seat row (`consentAt`, `source`, `locale`) to the attacker's address.
- The move runs before the S9-02 intent check, so it also runs on the parked "No soy yo" path. Nothing checks that the account ever owned the old address.
- None of this depends on `UNUSED_ACCOUNT_PURGE_ENABLED` or `LEGAL_PAGES_PUBLISHED`.
- Fix:
  - In `requestEmailChange`, record the old address in `app_metadata` through the service role (`admin.updateUserById(user.id, { app_metadata: { email_change_from } })`), after `updateUser` succeeds. Only the service role can write `app_metadata`.
  - Read it only from `data.user.app_metadata`, and clear it there.
  - Ignore `user_metadata` completely.
  - Add a test where `user_metadata` carries another address and nothing moves.

### Medium (they matter once the switch is on, or for the legal text)

**M1. The 12 months are counted from the auth row, not from the reservation or the latest request.**
- Where: `lib/unusedAccountPurge.ts:87`.
- Scenario: someone asked for a link 13 months ago and never clicked. Today they reserve a founding seat. `reserveFoundingSeat`, then `signInWithOtp` on the existing unconfirmed user, updates only `confirmation_sent_at` (it goes through GoTrue's signup branch: `isNewUser = !user.IsConfirmed()` in `magic_link.go`). 48 hours later the account is deleted together with a reservation that is 2 days old.
- Section 3 ("Si nunca llega a entrar, eliminamos esa cuenta sin usar y la reserva pasados 12 meses") reads as 12 months from the reservation, so it is false here.
- Fix (code): add a "blocks" condition for a `waitlist_entries` row with the same address and `createdAt >= cutoff`. Better, count the 12 months from `GREATEST(created_at, confirmation_sent_at, recovery_sent_at)` and the reservation date. Or change the text (proposal below).

**M2. Nothing re-checks the account between the transaction and the auth delete.**
- Where: `lib/unusedAccountPurge.ts:193`, `lib/unusedAccountPurge.ts:289`. The in-transaction re-check is a plain `SELECT` (no lock). After the commit, `deleteAuthUser` runs unconditionally, up to 3 HTTP attempts with no backoff.
- Walk: a first sign-in can land between the re-check and `deleteUser`. Links cannot be used in that window: every candidate's last access email is more than 48 hours old, and a link lives at most 24 hours. So in practice the only path is a Google sign-in for the same address.
  - What happens is worse than the brief's premise: the auth user is deleted, not kept.
  - That person's JWT stops working at the first `getUser()` (`requireUser` throws `not_authenticated`). They sign in again and get a fresh account.
  - Nothing of anyone else's is lost, and the window is milliseconds on an account dormant for 12 months.
- The opposite partial state (profile deleted, auth user kept and now confirmed) is recoverable: `requireUser` (`lib/auth.ts:40`) upserts the profile. Only the group roster links already cleared are not restored, because `handle_new_user` fires only on INSERT.
- Fix: right before `deleteAuthUser`, re-run `AUTH_ELIGIBLE_WHERE AND id = $4`. If the account no longer qualifies, skip the auth delete and log `unusedAccount.signedInDuringPurge` (ids only).
- Mutation M4 below shows no test covers the in-transaction re-check.

**M3. Section 10 says a reservation is kept if the person signed in, but the orphan path can delete it.**
- Where: `lib/unusedAccountPurge.ts:163` (orphan path).
- Text: "Si usted llegó a entrar, la reserva se conserva mientras exista la cuenta".
- Cases where a reservation of a person who did sign in has no auth user at its address:
  - email changes completed before this deploy;
  - a move that failed or returned `target_exists`;
  - someone who reserved with one address and signs in with Google under another;
  - a reservation where GoTrue refused the send (429 after `beforeSend` created the row).
- Each of these is deleted after 12 months once the switch is on. The text also never mentions orphan reservations.
- Fix: change the text (below), or keep orphan rows whose address appears in `auth.users.raw_user_meta_data->>'email_change_from'` or in an identity. In any case the owner should know the orphan count before switching on (R1).

**M4. "pasados 12 meses" is only true if the switch is on in time and the backlog is small.**
- Where: `lib/features.ts` (default off), `UNUSED_ACCOUNT_BATCH=50`.
- The text is false whenever the switch is off when the oldest never-used account turns 12 months, or when more than 50 are due per day.
- The runbook's item 3.4 covers publication. Nothing alerts when accounts become due while the pages are already published: `unusedAccount.switchOff` is only a `logWarn`.
- Fix: keep the runbook gate, and use "a partir de los 12 meses" style wording (proposal below).

### Low

- **L1. Rehearsal can delete real reservations.** Runbook step 5 turns on the orphan path as well: up to 500 real reservations could be deleted on the first run. Step 4 checks only `unusedAccounts.due`. See R1.
- **L2. The tests never prove each line of defence alone.** Removing either the SQL "empty account" filter or the in-transaction plan check still prevents the deletion; the other line catches it. The only test that fails is on counts. No test inserts a blocking row after selection, so the in-transaction check is never the deciding gate. See the mutations below.
- **L3. Empty-string emails.** SQL `u.email IS NOT NULL` vs the pure `!u.email`: an account with `email = ''` is eligible in SQL and `no_email` in TypeScript. The app has no phone auth, so the case does not arise today. Fix: add `AND u.email <> ''`.
- **L4. No full-row comparison in the "other person's rows" test.** The DB test checks counts and a few fields, not byte-identical rows. Q's `tasting_group_members` row is checked on `email`, `displayName` and `userId` only. Add a before/after `to_jsonb` snapshot of every row the other person owns.
- **L5. Profile-flag test misses the avatar.** The DB test has no `avatarUrl` patch (unit-tested only).
- **L6. A deleted account loses its group links.** After a deleted unused account signs in again, the roster rows are re-linked by `handle_new_user` (a new INSERT), so that case is fine. But if the auth delete fails for good and the person then signs in to the surviving auth user, the roster rows stay unlinked.
- **L7. A link sent mid-run can be broken.** A link requested in the seconds between the re-check and `deleteUser` arrives dead. The person requests a new one and gets a fresh account; acceptable.

## Point 1: can the rule select a real account?

| Kind of account | Selectable? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| (a) Email link | No | `verify.go`: `magiclink`/`signup` call `user.Confirm(tx)` (sets `email_confirmed_at`), then `issueRefreshToken` (`tokens/service.go` sets `LastSignInAt`). Done server-side at verify. |
| (b) 6-digit code | No | Same `/verify` path via `verifyOtp({type:"email"})`, which is rewritten to `signup`/`magiclink`. |
| (c) Google | No | The OAuth identity confirms the email and issues tokens (sets `last_sign_in_at`). |
| (d) Anonymous guest | No | `is_anonymous = true`. |
| (d) Guest who claimed | No | The surviving row is the account they signed into through the normal login, so it is confirmed and has `last_sign_in_at`. `mergeGuestData` deletes the anonymous row. |
| (e) Link 13 months ago, new request today | Not for 48 h; then yes if they don't click | A repeat `signInWithOtp` for an unconfirmed user takes GoTrue's signup branch and updates `confirmation_sent_at`, never `recovery_sent_at`. The 48 h guard exceeds the 24 h link life, so a live link is never broken, apart from the L7 race. If the app budget or GoTrue's 429 refuses the send, nothing is sent and nothing is updated, so no link exists to break. | (h) Super-admin / AI admins | No | Matched on `lower(btrim(email))`. With the env unset, the code fallback address applies; independently, they have signed in. |
| (i) Ephemeral verify + `setSession` (S9-02) | No | Confirmation and `last_sign_in_at` are written by GoTrue during `/verify`, whichever client keeps the tokens. Even "No soy yo" leaves the account confirmed, which errs safe. |

## Legal sentences that are not exactly true (proposals for the architect)

1. **Privacy section 3** ("Si nunca llega a entrar, eliminamos esa cuenta sin usar y la reserva pasados 12 meses."), because of M1 and M4. If the code keeps its current rule:
   > «…Si nunca llega a entrar, eliminamos esa cuenta sin usar, junto con la reserva, una vez transcurridos {N} meses desde que se creó la cuenta, aunque después haya pedido otro enlace.»

   If the code is fixed per M1:
   > «…una vez transcurridos {N} meses desde su última reserva o solicitud de acceso.»
2. **Privacy section 10**, second sentence ("Si usted llegó a entrar, la reserva se conserva mientras exista la cuenta…"), because of M3:
   > «Si usted llegó a entrar con la misma dirección de la reserva, la reserva se conserva mientras exista la cuenta y se elimina al suprimirla. Las reservas cuya dirección no corresponde a ninguna cuenta se eliminan pasados {N} meses desde la reserva.»
3. **Terms section 4**, "los resultados del grupo se muestran cuando la cata termina". Under the current code (`canViewGroupResults`) the group's results also show once everyone has submitted, before any close, so the sentence is ambiguous. Under the future rule it is true. Wording that holds under both:
   > «los resultados del grupo se muestran a los participantes al final de la cata, según las condiciones de cierre de cada cata.»

   Or, precise for the future rule only:
   > «…cuando el organizador cierra la cata o llega su fecha de cierre.»
4. **Privacy section 10, cookie row**: for an email change, `updateUser` saves the session at once and `_saveSession` deletes the verifier in the same request, so the cookie never outlives the request. It is accurate enough as written. Optionally drop «cambia su correo».

Co-cuppers (section 4) matches `CoCupperCard` and the scoped query. The AI section matches the chat tools. The English mirrors the Spanish in every changed sentence, with no stronger or weaker meaning.
